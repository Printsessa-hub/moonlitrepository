A SPRING NIGHT YOU CAN'T ESCAPE · Chapter 02

Marriage Certificate

As the curtain was drawn shut once more, the relaxed and comfortable atmosphere in the VIP room vanished. Even the designer and manager had reverted to their formulaic smiles, and the room felt as stifling as a pool of stagnant water.

With He Jingting’s sudden arrival, Jiang Yuan tactfully slipped away.

Even after the long French-style gown was put back on, Shu Cheng still felt as if she were in a dream.

He had left after taking his measurements—why had he suddenly come back?

This was a strapless style, revealing her collarbone and a large expanse of fair skin on her chest.

She hadn’t noticed it when she tried it on earlier, but now that she realized He Jingting was standing at the door, she felt self-conscious and instinctively reached up to pull the fabric on her chest tightly upward.

“Be careful,” the designer whispered.

To prevent any accidental exposure, the neckline of a strapless wedding dress is designed to be tight; Shu Cheng pulled too hard, and the stiff metal clasp instantly left a red mark where it pressed against her skin.

She was so nervous she didn’t even feel the pain.

Shu Cheng stepped out of the fitting room cautiously. He Jingting was still sitting where he’d been before, his left leg casually draped over his right knee. He scanned her from head to toe, his gaze finally settling on her chest.

The white light surrounding them was bright and translucent, and the faint red mark newly etched into her snow-white skin stood out more than she’d imagined.

The air remained still for a moment. He motioned for the manager to bring the catalog and, furrowing his brow, flipped through the pages one by one: “Try this one on.”

His fingertip tapped lightly on the paper, and the manager immediately hurried off to prepare it, while he continued to look through the catalog.

Shu Cheng had delicate, innocent features. The wedding gowns she’d tried on at first were mostly dreamy, ethereal white veils that highlighted her lively and adorable charm. But the one He Jingting chose was the complete opposite—a form-fitting, backless mermaid-style gown with a flowing skirt.

At first glance, it seemed completely mismatched.

But as the skirt flared out, her long hair was pulled back low, and the V-shaped white lace extended to her waist, perfectly revealing her delicate, slender collarbones. Her figure was exquisitely proportioned, adding a touch of allure and femininity. She looked like a completely different person.

When Shu Cheng stepped out of the fitting room, even the manager standing nearby’s eyes lit up.

It suited her perfectly.

He Jingting’s eye for style was truly sharp; he hadn’t obscured her pure, natural charm, but rather added a few ripples to a clear pond, making her even more alluring.

Under his intense gaze, she lowered her eyes slightly, hesitant to meet his.

“Hmm.” He Jingting tilted his chin upward, seeming reasonably satisfied. “Next one.”

Shu Cheng was like a doll playing dress-up, letting the designer and manager change her into one wedding gown after another, then lightly touch up her makeup and hairstyle.

Just a moment ago, she was fooling around with Jiang Yuan, and it took her half an hour just to try on one dress.

Now, with eight outfits, the entire dress fitting was wrapped up in less than an hour thanks to this efficient, streamlined process.

During the presentation, He Jingting stepped out to make two phone calls, with Secretary Zhong staying by his side the whole time; it seemed he had some urgent business to attend to, as he never put his phone down.

Only when Shu Cheng walked in front of him would he casually glance at her a couple of times.

Finally, He Jingting made the final decision: “Let’s go with these for now, and then pick out two sets of formal wear for the best man and maid of honor.”

The designer, showing great tact, brought over the tablet to confirm the designs with Shu Cheng: “Once everything is ready, we’ll invite you back for another fitting and any necessary adjustments.”

The four photos on the screen all featured traditional white lace designs, even though that mermaid-style gown was clearly the prettiest, He Jingting didn’t choose it. Shu Cheng was a little puzzled, but since he’d made the choice, she naturally didn’t dare to object.

So, without even taking the tablet, she obediently nodded without hesitation: “Thank you, any of them are fine with me.”

“Please wait a moment; I’ll have you try on some high heels right away.”

The designer’s figure gradually disappeared down the hallway, the manager brought out a fresh round of refreshments, and the VIP lounge once again fell into silence, so quiet you could hear a pin drop.

He Jingting looked down at his phone; the silence made the tension even more oppressive. Shu Cheng stood stiffly at the entrance to the fitting room, not wanting to get involved at all, and awkwardly pretended to be invisible.

But the designer was taking a long time coming back, and she could sense that he had glanced her way twice, looking rather displeased.

Is he having a bad day at work, and even his breathing is wrong?

Shu Cheng’s mind raced, trying to come up with an excuse to slip outside and hide for a while.

Just as she lifted the hem of her skirt, she heard He Jingting’s long, slender knuckles tapping lightly on the armrest of the sofa: “Come sit down.”

This “kindness” sent a chill down her spine. She steeled herself and walked over and slowly sat down at the far end of the sofa, tucking in the slightly loose hem of her skirt.

The girl’s long eyelashes drooped, and her pink lips pressed together slightly, giving away her nervousness.

“Sit closer.” He Jingting’s expression darkened completely; his voice wasn’t loud, but it carried an oppressive aura of impending storm. “Do you think I’m going to eat you?”

Shu Cheng’s fingertips trembled slightly, sinking completely into the layers of sheer fabric.

　　

She shifted closer to him and swallowed hard: “I was afraid my skirt might… brush against you.”

Suddenly, her wrist was yanked abruptly—

He Jingting’s movement came out of nowhere; Shu Cheng lost her balance and nearly fell onto him.

His fingers were icy cold as he gripped her slender wrist and stroked it leisurely, as if toying with an ant that couldn’t escape his grasp.

On her wrist was a delicate bracelet studded with tiny diamonds, glinting in the lamplight.

“A Sky Full of Stars?”

He narrowed his eyes slightly, feigning ignorance.

It was an uncomfortable position; they were standing so close that the tip of her nose barely brushed his shoulder. She could smell the cool, sandalwood scent of his perfume, which made Shu Cheng’s heart race.

“Hmm…”

She hadn’t expected him to recognize it—it was a new autumn collection from her personal brand, Vanstar.

He Jingting’s fingertips gently traced the palm of her hand, where a three-finger-wide scar lay—faint in color, yet etched into the lines of her palm, as if it were a mark that had never faded over the years.

He said casually, “You’ll be the one to design all the jewelry for the wedding.”

Shu Cheng paused in surprise.

With this wedding drawing the attention of the masses and swarming with media, he wasn’t choosing high-end luxury jewelry to adorn the occasion. Instead, he was handing this prime advertising spot over to her?

“Do I need to pay you a separate design fee?” He Jingting curved the corners of his lips, watching her with interest.

Shu Cheng quickly shook her head: “No need.”

"Then have my secretary take you to pick out a few valuable gems. Take off those little diamonds and put some more effort into our wedding…” As he spoke, his gaze swept visibly over her neck and ears, “Don’t let anyone think Yunshang has gone bankrupt.”

His breath seemed to brush against her ear, sending shivers down her spine.

If this went on much longer, Shu Cheng really would be unable to breathe. She tried to pull her wrist free, but his grip only tightened.

He asked, “Did you hear me?”

She swallowed hard. “I will…”

Seeing how compliant she was, he finally took pity on her and released his grip.

“Move in by the 11th.”

Shu Cheng didn’t understand: “What?”

“Do you need me to arrange a moving company for you?” He Jingting shot her a cold glance, his tone leaving no room for argument. “I don’t want a scandal about us living apart right after we’re married.”

She lowered her gaze, struggling to suppress her inner resistance to this reasonable demand, and nodded slightly.

“No need… I don’t have much stuff; I’ll move it myself.”

“Mm.” He didn’t press the issue further and casually looked away.

*

That evening, He Jingting sent her an address.

Yujiang Mansion—the most luxurious high-rise complex along the riverfront—was certainly in keeping with his typically flamboyant style.

Shu Cheng stared at the short line of text, sighed, and buried her head in the soft pillow.

A snow-white kitten leaped up lightly onto the bed; its clear, translucent blue eyes were as beautiful as glass beads, and it let out a soft, affectionate “meow.”

“Good girl, Tuantuan, what… should we do?”

As far as she could recall, He Jingting had asthma and had suffered attacks more than once while staying with the Shu family.

So many years had passed that Shu Cheng could no longer remember the specific details, but now, with his decisive and energetic demeanor and his daily schedule filled with business banquets and drinking parties, he didn’t seem like someone who’d been suffering from a chronic illness for a long time.

Perhaps he’d been cured, but would he agree to let her bring the kitten to stay with him?

Just as she was pondering this, her phone suddenly vibrated again.

She thought He Jingting had sent another request, her heart leapt into her throat. After rummaging through her phone for a moment, she realized it was a new message on WeChat.

Shu Lin: [Chengcheng, come home for lunch tomorrow.]

Her father calling her back to the family home at this time must have something to do with the arranged marriage.

Shu Cheng didn’t want to cause any more trouble, so she made up an excuse: 【I’ve had a cold these past couple of days. I don’t want to spread it to you guys, so I won’t be coming back.】

Sure enough, in less than three minutes, Shu Lin replied with a long message.

[How come you’re suddenly sick? Take care of yourself and stop rushing to the office every day. Nothing is more important right now than preparing for the wedding. Xiao He and our family are meant to be, you need to seize this opportunity, rein in your temper, and be more proactive. Invite him over to our place sometime; after all, the old family home is where he grew up.]

[Oh, and ask Xiao He when that investment will come through?]

Shu Cheng set her phone down and closed her eyes in despair.

Her mother had passed away when she was very young. Shu Lin quickly remarried and had children; he didn’t even show up when her grandmother was gravely ill—he was on vacation overseas with his wife and children and merely sent a sum of money to brush the matter aside.

Ever since then, that old family home had hardly been a “home” anymore.

She could already picture her father’s sycophantic smile as he openly and covertly urged her to cling to He Jingting, that “big tree”—so the Shu family could bask in his shadow. As for the methods, they would inevitably involve seduction, beauty, and her body…

She was just an unappreciated daughter, but after raising her for over twenty years, she was finally getting something in return.

Over the next few days, Shu Cheng worked overtime at her studio. She temporarily turned down all commercial orders, delegated miscellaneous tasks to her assistant, and devoted herself entirely to creating the jewelry for the wedding.

This wedding was indeed a great opportunity to promote the brand. She obtained detailed design sketches from the bridal shop, carefully selected rough gemstones, created design drawings, built 3D models, and polished and set the stones—even with her team’s assistance, the entire process was time-consuming and labor-intensive.

She sat there all day long, and it wasn’t until the sun had set that Shu Cheng finally emerged from the jewelry workshop, exhausted.

Her assistant poked her head in: “Ah, Mr. Lu is waiting for you.”

Mr. Lu?

As she looked up, that unexpected visitor immediately caught her eye.

“Shu Cheng.”

Lu Siyan stood a few steps away, the gentle light of the setting sun falling on his tall, slender shoulders. He gave a faint smile, though his expression held a hint of melancholy.

“Would you like to grab some dinner?”

The last time they’d seen each other was during the Lantern Festival last year, when she’d visited the Lu family home. Lu Siyan had just been about to leave for a business trip to Russia, and the two had merely nodded to each other as they passed each other at the Lu residence. Later, she’d even received a souvenir from him—two adorable little Russian matryoshka dolls.

Half an hour later, the two of them were seated in a private Cantonese restaurant. The Longjing tea gave off a delicate aroma, and a few slender leaves sank slowly to the bottom of the cup.

“I’m truly sorry about the engagement. I only found out today that you’d visited the old family home,” Lu Siyan said straight to the point, his tone as gentle and polite as she remembered. “The old man wasn’t feeling well recently; he may have been negligent. It certainly wasn’t his intention…”

Shu Cheng was momentarily taken aback, unsure why he had suddenly brought up the matter.

After a brief pause, he asked gently, “Are those news reports true?”

Busy with work all day, she had barely checked the internet.

The moment she opened her phone, dozens of news articles popped up on the home screen, along with over a dozen missed calls from her father and close friends.

As soon as Shu Cheng saw the headlines, she gasped—

[Former Engagement Vanishes into Thin Air! Yun Shang’s High-Profile Marriage to the Shu Family Shocks South City (Nanshi). Where Will the Lu Family’s Second Young Master Find Love?]

The marriage alliance between the He and Shu families had long been the talk of the town, and the Lu family, whom she had once been rumored to be engaged, had also been thrown into the spotlight.

Major media outlets were scrambling to cover the story, but public opinion seemed to have been artificially manipulated, clearly favoring Yunshang. Some reporters even dug up old stories, making a big deal out of the fact that He Jingting had once stayed with the Shu family, spinning romantic tales of childhood sweethearts and a marriage to repay a debt of gratitude.

As soon as the engagement news broke, Yunshang Group’s stock price rose steadily until the Hong Kong stock market closed that evening.

She closed her eyes briefly and turned off the screen, unwilling to look any longer.

They had clearly agreed to wait until the time was right and discuss it together before announcing the engagement, yet He Jingting had beaten her to the punch and reaped all the benefits.

“I’m sorry, it’s all just unscrupulous media making things up and dragging you into all this nonsense,” Shu Cheng said guiltily. “I’ll have someone take those stories down as soon as possible.”

“It’s okay. These rumors aren’t important.” Lu Siyan, however, seemed to have misunderstood her words; his expression relaxed as he gallantly poured her some tea. “It’s also my fault for being so busy these past few years. Once I get back, I’ll have a proper talk with my grandfather and finalize the engagement as soon as possible…”

Shu Cheng was momentarily lost in thought. If only he’d said that sooner.

“It’s true.” She lowered her eyes, cutting off any last shred of hope: “Those aren’t rumors. He Jingting and I have already registered our marriage.”

His hand trembled, and the scalding tea instantly spilled over the rim of the cup, soaking a large patch of the tablecloth.
