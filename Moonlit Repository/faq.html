Frequently Asked Questions

What is this site?

A personal blog and translation archive for sharing translated works, reading notes, updates, and submissions.

How do I add a new chapter?

Copy an existing chapter HTML file, rename it, replace the text, and add its link to the novel table of contents.

Can I submit corrections?

Yes. Include the chapter and phrase in question when possible.
