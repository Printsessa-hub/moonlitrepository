This site is designed to be a quiet reading space: novels are separated into projects, chapters have focused reading pages, and ordinary blog posts live alongside translation notes and announcements.

WHAT TO FIND HERE

Translations are organized by novel, with a table of contents and chapter navigation. Posts hold reading journals, translator notes, announcements, and other entries.

Some stories travel far before they find a place to rest.

ABOUT THE TRANSLATOR

The translator is the one responsible for the translations and website update. Please do have some patience if the website is buffering or anything. It means that there is an ongoing update of the website for possible chapter updates. Translations will be posted and updated on the scheduled day for posting of translations. It is possible to have 2 updates per week but not guaranteed.
