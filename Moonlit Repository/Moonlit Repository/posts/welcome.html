ABOUT THE TRANSLATOR

The translator is the one responsible for the translations and website update. Please do have some patience if the website is buffering or anything. It means that there is an ongoing update of the website for possible chapter updates.

The translator has a full-time job in real life and will do its best to update continuously the ongoing projects. For now, there are a few translated chapters to be posted according to schedule.

Translations will be posted and updated on the scheduled day for posting of translations. It is possible to have 2 chapter updates or more per week but not guaranteed. It depends on the total quantity of translated chapters available.
