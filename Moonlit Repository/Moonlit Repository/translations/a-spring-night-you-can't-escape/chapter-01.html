A SPRING NIGHT YOU CAN'T ESCAPE · Chapter 01

The Wedding Dress

It was an early autumn afternoon, and the southern part of the city was scorching hot, with the sun shining so brightly it was blinding.

The traffic on the bridge across the river was at a standstill; cars moved forward slowly with the flow of traffic. As the minutes ticked by, Shu Cheng couldn’t help but feel anxious.

It is 1:10 PM. There were only twenty minutes left until her scheduled time for her dress fitting.

But the bridal shop was still at least a dozen kilometers away, not to mention the traffic jam on the bridge stretched as far as the eye could see.

If she could turn back time, she would definitely have rescheduled that client from earlier that morning. The one who insisted on cutting a three-carat natural diamond into twelve small stones and arranging them to form his wife’s lucky number.

Shu Cheng sighed and pressed her forehead against the car window; the air conditioning was on full blast, and the glass felt ice-cold.

The driver, a cheerful and kind middle-aged woman, seemed to sense her restlessness: “Young lady, are you going to try on a wedding dress? When’s the wedding?”

In the rearview mirror, the girl gazed out the window, her long eyelashes casting a faint tea-brown hue in the sunlight, making her eyes appear even clearer. She looked well-behaved, with a hint of a student-like innocence, hardly the age one would expect for someone getting married.

But the destination on the order was indeed a bridal shop, located in the most expensive part of the city center.

“Um…” Shu Cheng didn’t want to talk about it, so she gave a faint smile. “Sometime in the second half of the year.”

“Don’t worry about it. Just let your husband know—there’s a pileup up ahead, and who knows how long we’ll be stuck in traffic,” the woman said, striking up a friendly conversation.

“Newlyweds are supposed to be enjoying their honeymoon phase—what’s a little wait?”

Shu Cheng forced a slight smile, but on the inside she felt a sense of loss and confusion about the marriage and future that lay ahead.

Love is certainly a beautiful thing. But from now on, those two words would have nothing to do with her.

As for the man she was about to marry, she had no right—and didn’t dare—to make him wait.

The sound of police sirens grew closer, as traffic bypassed the scene of the rear-end collision, the road opened up, and the pace finally returned to normal.

Suddenly, her phone vibrated. A news alert popped up on the screen.

[Headline: It’s Official! Yunshang Acquires Stake in HC Medical; Signing Ceremony in Berlin Concludes Successfully.]

Shu Cheng stared at it for a few seconds, then tapped the screen with her fingertip. The homepage displayed a photo from the event.

Amid the crowd, a tall, upright figure stood directly under the spotlight. The man exuded an exceptionally cold aura, with handsome and sculpted features, giving the illusion of mixed heritage. His pupils were an intense, deep black, and the outer corners of his eyes curved slightly upward, outlining an innate aloofness and sharpness—as if absorbing all light and noise, causing one to hold their breath involuntarily.

Even through the screen, a faint chill seemed to radiate from him.

Shu Cheng swiped quickly past the photo; when she reached the bottom of the page, a flood of related news articles appeared.

Her breath hitched, and she immediately turned off the screen.

But the dizzying array of sensational headlines from those small media outlets refused to fade, feeling like a wad of cotton lodged in her chest.

[Shocking Upheaval in a Wealthy Family! Yunshang’s Eldest Son Stops at Nothing to Seize Power, Personally Sending His Younger Brother to Prison.]

[“The Illegitimate Son’s Revenge”? A Ruthless Purge of His Own Brother; a Shocking Twist in the Battle for a 10-Billion Fortune.]

In the news reports, the cold-blooded and ruthless leader sitting at the top of the conglomerate was the very man Shu Cheng was set to marry in half a month.

He Jingting.

He had once been raised by the Shu family for several years as an illegitimate son, but they had very little interaction. Shu Cheng’s memories of him were nothing but scattered fragments—none of them pleasant.

Now that the Shu family is in decline, they have no choice but to rely on a political marriage to preserve their century-old legacy. She and the family’s reputation have become pawns, offered to Yunshang Group as a guarantee.

The sound of police sirens grew closer, traffic bypassed the scene of the rear-end collision, the road opened up, and the speed finally returned to normal.

But no matter what, she wouldn’t make it in time for their appointment.

Leaving He Jingting to wait for her—the one who was late… At this thought, Shu Cheng’s brow twitched.

After struggling with her thoughts for a while, she finally opened her contacts and tapped on a number labeled “He.”

How should she address him?

The term “Big Brother” she’d called him as a child was clearly inappropriate; “President He” felt too formal and distant. She wasn’t bold enough to call him by his first name.

After editing it back and forth, Shu Cheng finally sent the message without any title:

【I’m really sorry, there’s a traffic jam due to a traffic accident on the bridge across the river. I might be a little late, about twenty minutes.】

No reply.

Shu Cheng didn’t know if he’d seen it, so she could only wait and hope for better luck with the traffic lights after she got off the bridge.

Even on a weekday afternoon, the traffic in the city center didn’t decrease. With the constant stop-and-go along the way, she arrived nearly half an hour late.

The destination had no house number; an elegant European-style gate was nestled beneath the sycamore trees. By the time the car pulled over to the side of the road, Shu Cheng’s palms were covered in a thin layer of sweat.

The manager, who had been waiting for some time, immediately came forward:

“Good afternoon, Mrs. He. Please come in.”

Stepping inside felt like entering a tranquil little estate, with roses planted everywhere; bathed in dappled sunlight, the atmosphere was brimming with romance.

The entire store had been reserved exclusively for them. While Manager Li smiled and introduced the history and craftsmanship of their wedding gowns, he led her to the most private VIP suite on the top floor.

“These are all the brand’s signature pieces. Please feel free to select a few that catch your eye to try on, and our designer will then create a custom gown tailored to your style and preferences.”

In the vast, spacious room, the lighting made the wedding gowns on both sides shimmer, and a faint scent of jasmine lingered in the air.

Shu Cheng glanced toward the lounge area, where an exquisite afternoon tea set was arranged on the coffee table, but the sofa remained empty.

The cakes and macarons on the Ferris wheel-shaped tiered stand showed no signs of having been touched, but there were two coffee cups—one on the left and one on the right—with the one on the right clearly only half full.

Shu Cheng felt a pang of emptiness in her heart and asked softly, “Has he started trying them on yet?”

The fitting room right in the center was half-open; she looked around but couldn’t find any other doors.

Manager Li’s expression flickered with confusion for a moment before settling into a polite smile: “Mr. He’s four suits have all been measured. A representative will deliver them to your home next week.”

His words were tactful, but the implication was clear: He Jingting had already finished trying on the suits and left.

Shu Cheng looked bewildered. “How long ago did he leave?”

“About twenty minutes ago.”

Soon, a cup of freshly brewed Australian white coffee was brought to the table. She sat down and flipped through the wedding dress catalog. Out of the corner of her eye, she saw that the ice cubes in the cup of coffee he hadn’t finished had partially melted, floating sporadically on the surface of the brown liquid.

He Jingting was swamped with work, so it was only natural that he didn’t have time to wait even half an hour. This wedding—and indeed, their marriage—was nothing more than a facade for the He and Shu families.

Not having to face him actually made Shu Cheng breathe a sigh of relief.

Her eyes fell on the exquisite and tempting French pastries on the table; she casually picked up a fork, cut off a slice of cake, and popped it into her mouth.

The mousse was velvety, the cheese rich, with a sweet-and-sour lemon aroma.

It was exactly her favorite flavor.

Shu Cheng had a sweet tooth, and paired with some floral tea to cut through the richness, she polished off the entire slice.

According to the wedding schedule, she was supposed to change into four wedding gowns. But after trying on just one, she received a call from her best friend.

Jiang Yuan was so surprised she couldn’t close her mouth: “What? He actually left you there all alone to try on wedding dresses?”

“I was the one who was late…”

Before Shu Cheng could finish her sentence, she heard her friend exclaim excitedly on the other end: “How can you make such a once-in-a-lifetime decision on a whim? Wait for me to come and give you some advice!”

Twenty minutes later, Jiang Yuan sat down in the VIP lounge, her jaw dropping at the opulent decor:

“Even the basic ready-to-wear wedding dresses from this brand cost over 100,000 yuan; the haute couture ones must be well over a million.”

Shu Cheng lowered her eyelashes, hiding a hint of resignation: “After all, this wedding is a matter of prestige for Yunshang Group.”

They were marrying her to create a beautiful love story—how could it not be a grand affair?

Seeing her such a negative reaction to the wedding, Jiang Yuan hesitated, then asked, “...Is it because of Lu Siyan?”

“No, he and I are really just friends.”

The Shu and Lu families had always been on good terms; when they were children, their parents had even arranged a betrothal.

Lu Siyan was handsome and refined, gentle as jade—truly a suitable match for marriage. But as they grew up, their paths diverged, and they had little contact; naturally, there was no question of any “romantic feelings” between them.

“Then why haven’t you told him about the marriage alliance with the He family?”

“We signed an agreement to wait until the time is right before making an official announcement.”

In truth, Shu Cheng understood that the verbal engagement from back then was likely no longer valid—after the Shu family’s construction project ran into trouble, they had visited the Lu family to seek help, hinting that they wanted to marry as soon as possible.

But Old Mr. Lu’s response was evasive; he seemed unwilling to acknowledge it. She knew full well the saying, “When the wall falls, everyone pushes,” so she wasn’t particularly surprised.

Jiang Yuan had grown accustomed to her gentle nature and firmly believed she had given up true love for the sake of the family business:

“The He family certainly has clout, but neither my father nor Uncle Lu would stand idly by. You need to have confidence—we’ll definitely find a way through this difficult time. But marriage is a lifelong commitment; if you don’t want to marry…”

“It’s not that I don’t want to get married—how could I lose out by marrying into the He family? Besides, I don’t have anyone I like anyway…” Shu Cheng put on a smile, not wanting to worry her best friend. “Okay, come on, help me decide—which of these two styles suits me better?”

She changed the subject and pulled Jiang Yuan over to the wedding dress rack.

The two girls gathered together to pick out wedding gowns and bridesmaid dresses, trying on one set after another and taking photos. They quickly put their troubles behind them, and waves of laughter filled the room.

Before they knew it, the hour hand on the wall clock had turned round and round.

“Is the waistline on this one too tight?”

“It’s not tight—that’s exactly what makes your waist look slimmer. Who cares who the groom is? On your wedding day, you have to be the most beautiful!” Jiang Yuan was full of enthusiasm and picked out two matching outfits—a wedding dress and a bridesmaid dress. “This set is gorgeous. You try it on first; I’ll change in the fitting room in the back.”

After pulling the curtain closed, the designer helped Shu Cheng slip into this elegant, train-style gown.

The bodice was embroidered with hundreds of delicate rhinestones that sparkled like a starry sky under the lights, making the layers of light, white tulle look ethereal and dreamlike.

She already had a baby-faced appearance, and the faint blush on her fair cheeks made her look like a little princess who had escaped from a forest castle.

The designer smiled appreciatively and said, “This piece looks like it was tailor-made for you.”

Shu Cheng gazed at her unique reflection in the mirror, and a wave of warmth welled up in her heart. So this is what it feels like to wear a wedding dress…

Just then, the manager’s hushed voice and footsteps drifted faintly from outside the fitting room.

It seemed Jiang Yuan had finished changing. Just as Shu Cheng was about to pull back the curtain, the designer stopped her:

“Just a moment—let me tighten the shoulders by an inch.”

“Yuanyuan, I’ll be done in a second.” She turned back to the mirror, her voice tinged with excitement. “This one is truly beautiful. You’re right, no matter who I marry, a wedding is a once-in-a-lifetime event.”

The designer deftly used a small pin to cinch the shoulders, adjusting them for the perfect fit:

“Mrs. He, please.”

The manager pulled the curtain open from behind, and Shu Cheng, eager to share the moment with her best friend, turned around with a beaming smile:

“Which do you think is better, this one or that French-style dress?”

Before she could finish her question, her rising tone died in her throat.

Directly across from the fitting room, a man leaned casually against a leather sofa. His well-built, sturdy frame was clad in a subtly patterned suit; his hands were folded across his chest, and the platinum watch face peeking from his cuffs cast a faint, cool glow.

His eyes were dark and deep, his gaze sharp as a drawn blade, locking onto her delicate, white silhouette.

Shu Cheng shuddered uncontrollably, her smile frozen on her face.

She looked as startled as a little rabbit that had stumbled upon a predator, ready to flee at any moment.

He Jingting frowned imperceptibly, his thin lips pressed into a straight line, as if suppressing an emotion on the verge of bursting free.

“How did you…”

She snapped back to her senses, her fingertips gently tugging at the hem of her skirt.

Had he heard what she’d just said?

He Jingting scrutinized her with a piercing gaze, then suddenly stood up and approached her silently.

Tall and imposing, he blocked the light behind him, casting an oppressive shadow.

The atmosphere was as taut as a bowstring, and Shu Cheng’s heartbeat suddenly slowed: “I’m sorry, I was late today.”

“President He!”

Seeing from a distance that the man seemed on the verge of losing his temper, Jiang Yuan, holding the hem of her skirt, ran over in small steps and stepped sideways to block Shu Cheng.

She swallowed hard to steady her nerves and said, “Shu Cheng was late today because of me; she didn’t mean to.”

It was a clumsy lie; in her panic, she hadn’t even managed to come up with a specific excuse.

Shu Cheng froze for a moment, tugging slightly at Jiang Yuan’s sleeve, looking like some kind of pitiful little animal.

What did they think he was going to do?

He Jingting curled his lips into a cold smile, strode right past Jiang Yuan, and raised his hand toward Shu Cheng—

Her body went rigid, and she instinctively closed her eyes.

A gentle touch brushed against her hair, gliding lightly past.

He Jingting plucked a sparkling diamond from the long strand of hair clinging to her ear, glanced at the girl’s flustered expression, then turned and lazily sat back down on the sofa.

He left only one sentence: “Put it back on and see how it looks.”

Shu Cheng froze for a moment before realizing He Jingting was responding to the question she’d just asked.
