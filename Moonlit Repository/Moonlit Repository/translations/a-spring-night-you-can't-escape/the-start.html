A SPRING NIGHT YOU CAN'T ESCAPE · Description

The Start

Shu Cheng never would have dared to dream that she would marry the man she had always regarded as an older brother through an arranged marriage.

He Jingting, the head of the conglomerate, had an extremely handsome, piercing face. Tall and straight, mature and steady, it was hard for an innocent, naive girl not to fall for him.

A year into their marriage, Shu Cheng was like a pampered doll, wearing the beautiful clothes he liked, being held in his arms while she ate, kissed constantly, and indulged in his unbridled demands—yet even that wasn’t enough—

Beneath He Jingting’s perfect facade, a spine-chilling obsession and possessiveness gradually began to surface.

At a banquet, she twisted her ankle, and her childhood friend simply reached out to steady her.

That night, he frantically left red marks all over her body, even cruelly teasing her to the brink of climax, then making her promise to be his.

“Waaah… No, I don’t want to see him anymore.”

Shu Cheng sobbed for mercy, her face streaked with tears.

He Jingting’s ghostly black eyes flickered as he satisfiedly nibbled on her earlobe: “Be a good girl, or else… I have plenty of ways to make him disappear for good.”

Terrified, Shu Cheng left behind a signed divorce agreement and fled in panic to her European estate.

When she awoke, she discovered that all the doors and windows were locked.

Later, Shu Cheng finally divorced him and traveled to Italy for work, embracing a new life of freedom.

They met again at a charity gala. She was dancing with another handsome man, her skirt swirling gracefully.

He Jingting was still the center of attention—handsome and dignified—yet he treated her with such indifference that he might as well have been a stranger.

She lowered her gaze and breathed a sigh of relief.

It was best for both of them to part ways and move on.

Until that night, when Shu Cheng quietly returned to the mansion where they had once lived to retrieve some documents.

No sooner had she stepped into the dimly lit living room than she was swept into an embrace and kissed by a man who had just emerged from the bathroom.

Pills were scattered all over the floor, leaving a scene of utter chaos. He kissed her tenderly and devotedly, gently taking her inch by inch, nearly making Shu Cheng’s knees give way.

She mustered her last shred of reason to push him away.

She saw that He Jingting’s disheveled hair was soaking wet, his gaze unfocused, and his face eerily pale. His whole body suddenly shook violently, and he coughed up a spurt of scalding blood.

“Chengcheng, so this is how much it has to hurt… just to see you again.”

Lost in a hollow hallucination, his bloodstained lips pressed against hers once more, overflowing with love.
