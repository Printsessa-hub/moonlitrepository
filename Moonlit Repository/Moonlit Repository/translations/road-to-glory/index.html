The Crown Princess's Road to Glory

Original Title: 太子妃的荣华路

Author: 秀木成林

An Orphan Pushed Forward to Marry the Crown Prince By Her Own Aunt | Embarking on the Path of Exclusive Favor in the Eastern Palace and Rising Through the Ranks.

Synopsis:

First, she was orphaned at a young age due to the early death of her parents; second, she was pushed forward by her aunt, the Empress Consort, to marry the Crown Prince born to the former Empress.

Bystanders: We all know the Empress Consort's ambition to seize the Eastern Palace.

However, this doesn't affect her path to becoming the Crown Prince's favorite and rising through the ranks.
